The theme

Mayura

Named for the peacock — vehicle of Kartikeya, crown of Krishnaji, and an age-old symbol of devotion, beauty, and new beginnings.

In every feather, a thousand colours. In every note of the bansuri, a promise. Our wedding weekend is a royal celebration of love — steeped in tradition, lit by gold, and watched over by the Mayura.
